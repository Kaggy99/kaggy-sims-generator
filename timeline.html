Occult Chronology

The occult chronology distinguishes ancient supernatural peoples, later created beings, extraterrestrial species, and post-contact hybrid populations by the period in which each first becomes possible.

Exists by 1300

  • Angel, Demon, Dragon, Fairy, Genie, Ghost, Gargoyle, Goblin, Gorgon, Kitsune, Mermaid, Minotaur, Reaper, Revenant, Satyr, Vampire, Werewolf, Witch, Zombie, and Doppelganger.
  • Ghosts, Revenants, and Zombies are separate states: a Ghost is a soul without its body, a Revenant returns to its own body with identity intact, and a Zombie is usually a reanimated body with an absent or damaged identity.
  • Gargoyles are pre-alien created beings awakened through stonework and protective magic. Dragons are ancient and are not products of alien hybridization.

Later, but still pre-alien

  • Spellcasters and Druids emerge as distinct groups during the Early Modern period.
  • Dollettes and Plants become established during the Industrial period.
  • Servos become possible in the Modern age before alien contact.

After Alien Contact

  • Known alien species are Pyrrakai, Vyr’kesh, Xal’tarra, Veythaal, and Serynth.
  • Alien genetics mixing with humans and older occult bloodlines eventually produces Avian, Cottontail, Entomorph, Faunian, Orc, and Prowler populations.
  • The accepted post-contact taxonomy records Prowler as the feline-humanoid species and Cottontail as the rabbit-featured species.
  • By the far future, ancient occults, created beings, alien species, and alien-descended peoples coexist while humans become a protected ancestral minority.

Era Overview

After the 2300s, occult societies return to public life, stable hybrid populations emerge, off-world civilizations expand, and humans gradually become a protected ancestral minority.

Middle Future

  • 2405 to 2550, Broken Reconstruction Age, survival states become law-bound societies again.
  • 2550 to 2750, Occult Reemergence Age, old supernatural lineages return to public life.
  • 2750 to 3000, Hybrid Dawn Age, alien biology and old magic create stable new species.

Far Future

  • 3000 to 3350, Species Divergence Age, bloodline law, species politics, and occult houses dominate.
  • 3350 to 3600, Galactic Rebirth Age, colonies stabilize and off-world identity matters.
  • 3600 to 3825, Post-Human Accord Age, humans are rare and protected.
  • 3825 to 4000, Mythic Star Age, magic, alien science, machine life, and bloodline law merge.
